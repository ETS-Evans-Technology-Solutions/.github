# Evans Technology Solutions, LLC

**Practical technology solutions for modern businesses.**

Evans Technology Solutions, LLC (ETS) helps organizations plan, build, improve, and manage technology that supports real business needs.
We work across infrastructure, systems integration, automation, cloud and self-hosted platforms, and applied AI—bringing together the right tools in a way that is practical, maintainable, and designed to fit the organization using them.
Our focus is not technology for technology’s sake. It is creating systems that are reliable, understandable, and useful.

## How We Help

ETS works with businesses that need clearer technology direction, better-connected systems, or stronger technical foundations.

Our work includes:

- IT infrastructure planning and implementation
- Systems integration
- Workflow and process automation
- Cloud and hybrid infrastructure
- Local and self-hosted AI systems
- Technology discovery and planning
- Configuration, deployment, and documentation

## Our Approach

Technology should support the business—not become another problem to manage.

ETS takes a structured approach built around understanding the environment first, then designing solutions that fit the organization’s actual requirements.

Our work emphasizes:

- **Practical design** — solutions sized to the real need
- **Clear planning** — understanding requirements before implementation
- **Maintainability** — systems that can be supported and understood over time
- **Integration** — helping tools and platforms work together effectively
- **Documentation** — reducing dependency on undocumented knowledge
- **Flexibility** — using the right combination of local, cloud, and hybrid technologies

This approach allows us to support both traditional IT needs and newer technologies without losing sight of reliability, security, and business value.
